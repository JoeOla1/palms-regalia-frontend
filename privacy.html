Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect. When you use our contact or booking forms, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and any dates, guest count, or message you provide.

Why we collect it. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry, confirm room availability, and coordinate your stay.

How it's handled. Form submissions are delivered directly to our email via Web3Forms. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights. You can request that we delete any information you've submitted by contacting us directly.

If you have questions about this policy, reach out via our Contact page.
