About The Apartment

Space to actually live in, not just sleep in

Palms Regalia Luxury Apartment offers a spacious multi-bedroom apartment in Port Harcourt with air-conditioning, a kitchenette, and a balcony. Each unit features a private bathroom, a washing machine, and tiled floors — with a 24-hour front desk, daily housekeeping, and full-day security throughout your stay.

Guests can also enjoy an on-site bar, free WiFi, and an outdoor seating area with BBQ facilities. In-room extras include a mini-bar, microwave, and cable flat-screen TV.
